Carbonate Lab

Modern-inspired composite carbonate platform

Explore a conceptual teaching model inspired by several modern carbonate settings. The environments are combined, and spatial scales, processes and timescales are simplified. This is not a quantitatively validated geological simulation. Carbonate systems are complex, built by living organisms, chemistry and physical processes acting together, and no simplified model can capture all of that.

  1. Where does it form? Each numbered pin connects an environment with an illustrative sediment or rock type.
  2. How does it form, and what does it look like? Open a pin to watch it form in 3D, turn a hand specimen, and look at its thin section under the microscope, alongside real photographs.
  3. What is it called? Explore Dunham and Folk names using generated grain proportions and stated assumptions about the depositional texture.

Open Sea level to explore a simplified cycle of flooding and exposure. Try Classify to build a rock of your own. Model info keeps the scientific basis and limitations available as you explore.
