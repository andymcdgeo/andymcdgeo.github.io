Why Bore Lab

A log is usually shown as a finished picture, but it is really a record of when and how the rock was measured. Sensors trail the bit, gas and cuttings take time to reach surface, and mud filtrate changes the rock around the borehole while you drill. Bore Lab lets you drill a well and watch each of those effects happen, with every curve generated from the same earth and petrophysical model.

It is the second tool in the Labs series, after Pore Lab, which looks inside the rock at the pore scale.

Inspired by

The Labs series was inspired by Ryan Sael's The Plane of Focus, an interactive lens lab that explains camera focus by letting you turn the focus ring and watch the sharp plane move through a scene. It showed how much a well-lit, hands-on model can teach.
